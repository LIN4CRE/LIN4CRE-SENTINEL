# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, precise and evidence-led. Welcome constructive review, especially when it identifies unsafe behaviour, misleading claims or accessibility barriers. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, credential disclosure and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Security research behaviour

Test only systems you own or have current explicit permission to test. Follow exact programme scope, methods, rate limits, data-minimisation and disclosure rules. Do not use this project to harass, extort, disrupt, access unnecessary data or publish another person’s private information.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit/remove contributions and restrict participation when behaviour violates this policy or creates unsafe research risk. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner rather than exposing sensitive details in an issue.

## Attribution

This policy is a concise project-specific standard inspired by the Contributor Covenant principles of respect, empathy and accountability.
