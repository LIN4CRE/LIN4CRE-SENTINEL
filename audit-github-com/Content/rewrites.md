## “How it works” block

1. **Record the programme.** Keep canonical policy and scope links with the date you checked them.
2. **Review exact scope.** Approve the specific assets and methods before any authorised action.
3. **Keep evidence.** Record only what you observed; validation locks the evidence snapshot.
4. **Prepare and submit yourself.** SENTINEL helps organise a report; you decide whether and where to submit it.
5. **Count real outcomes.** Estimates never become earnings; only awarded and paid amounts enter the ledger.

## Features copy

### A research workflow you can inspect
Rank cash-only programmes using recorded inputs and transparent factors. Ineligible or incomplete opportunities stay visible as “needs review” rather than being silently promoted.

### Exact-scope approvals
Review the programme, assets, allowed methods, rate limits, expiry and plan hash before approval. Changes to the plan invalidate the prior approval.

### Evidence before claims
Keep reproduction steps, impact, evidence and remediation together. SENTINEL does not invent findings or claim that a report was accepted.

### Local-first operations
Your working database stays on your machine. Optional local Ollama support is off by default; models classify a limited set of intents and cannot issue arbitrary shell commands.

### Recovery you can verify
Create recovery points with integrity checks and hashes before consequential changes. Restore is explicit and revokes affected approvals.

## “For / not for” copy

**Designed for:** independent researchers who want a local, auditable way to organise programme scope, evidence, reports and actual outcomes.

**Not designed for:** unattended scanning, mass exploitation, automatic report submission, guaranteed earnings, or replacing a programme’s rules and human judgement.
